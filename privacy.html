Privacy & Data

SSC Archives — Maths (Geometry)

SSC Archives stores account and progress information needed to provide login and test features.

  • Progress may be stored locally in your browser and, where enabled, in the connected account database.
  • Backup files are created only when you choose Create Backup.
  • Do not share your backup file or account credentials with others.
  • Local saved profile and progress data can be removed using Delete Profile & Local Data.
  • Google Sign-In, when enabled, may provide basic Google account information needed for authentication.

SSC Archives does not ask for unnecessary Google profile information for test functionality.
