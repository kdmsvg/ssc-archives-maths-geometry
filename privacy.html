Privacy & Data

SSC Archives — Maths (Geometry)

Current version: 0.4.0

Last updated: 6 September 2026

Google Sign-In

  • Google is used to verify your Google account and authenticate your login.
  • SSC Archives uses the verified Google account email address for authentication and account matching.
  • SSC Archives does not receive or store your Google password.
  • Your Full Name is entered by you during SSC Archives account setup. It is not taken from your Google profile by this platform.
  • SSC Archives does not ask for unnecessary Google profile information for test functionality.

Account & Test Data

  • Your SSC Archives User ID, Full Name, account information and test progress may be stored to provide the platform's login and test features.
  • Progress may also be stored locally in your browser.
  • Bookmarks, answers, test history and related progress data may be stored as required for the test features.
  • Backup files are created only when you choose Create Backup.

Important Safety Notice

Never enter or share passwords, financial information, OTPs, identity documents, or other sensitive personal information on SSC Archives. If someone asks you to share such information through this platform, do not provide it.

Users are responsible for keeping their credentials and any backup files private and for not voluntarily submitting sensitive information that the platform does not request.

Testing Purpose

This current version is an integration/testing build. It is provided for testing and educational practice while the platform is being developed toward its final production release.

Data Removal

Where available, local saved profile and progress data can be removed using Delete Profile & Local Data. Account/server-side data handling may depend on the enabled backend configuration.
